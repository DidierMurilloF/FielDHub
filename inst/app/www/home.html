What you get

Field maps

Every plot with its entry, block and checks, in a clean style ready for reports.

Field books

Rows, columns, plot numbers and entries for every location, to download and load into your database.

Simulated data

A simulated response with the randomization, for teaching and for testing an analysis.

R functions

Every design is also an R function, so a script can reproduce what the app made.
