    Articles¶

    Each design has a guide that builds it twice: in the Shiny app and with its R function.

    Designs¶

    Unreplicated Designs¶

    • Single Diagonal Arrangement

      Unreplicated entries with checks on diagonals, in one field or split among several experiments.

      diagonal_arrangement()

    • Optimized Arrangement

      Unreplicated entries with checks placed at random and spread across the field.

      optimized_arrangement()

    • Augmented RCBD

      The same checks in every block, with unreplicated entries distributed among the blocks.

      RCBD_augmented()

    • Augmented Row-Column

      Unreplicated entries with every check once in each row or each column of the field.

      augmented_row_column()

    • Sparse Allocation

      Each entry is tested at a subset of the locations, in an unreplicated design at each one.

      sparse_allocation()

    Partially Replicated Designs¶

    • Single and Multi-Location p-rep

      Some entries are replicated and the rest appear once, at one or several locations.

      partially_replicated()

    • Optimized Multi-Location p-rep

      Optimizes how the copies of each entry are shared among locations, then builds a p-rep design at each one.

      multi_location_prep()

    Lattice Designs¶

    • Square Lattice

      Resolvable incomplete blocks for k² treatments in blocks of size k.

      square_lattice()

    • Rectangular Lattice

      Resolvable incomplete blocks for k(k + 1) treatments in blocks of size k.

      rectangular_lattice()

    • Alpha Lattice: alpha(0,1)

      Resolvable incomplete blocks for a flexible number of treatments and block sizes.

      alpha_lattice()

    Other Designs¶

    • Completely Randomized Design (CRD)

      Treatments assigned to plots completely at random.

      CRD()

    • Randomized Complete Block Designs (RCBD)

      Every treatment once in each complete block, at one or several locations.

      RCBD()

    • Latin Square Design (LSD)

      Every treatment once in each row and each column of a square.

      latin_square()

    • Latin Rectangle Design

      Randomized cyclic Latin rectangle.

      latin_rectangle()

    • Factorial Designs

      All combinations of the levels of two or more factors, as a CRD or an RCBD.

      full_factorial()

    • Split-Plot Design

      Two factors, one on whole plots and one on sub-plots.

      split_plot()

    • Split-Split-Plot Design

      Three factors, on whole plots, sub-plots and sub-sub-plots.

      split_split_plot()

    • Strip-Plot Design

      Two factors applied in horizontal and vertical strips that cross in each block.

      strip_plot()

    • Incomplete Blocks Design (IBD)

      Resolvable incomplete blocks for a chosen number of treatments, replicates and block size.

      incomplete_blocks()

    • Resolvable Row-Column Design (RRCD)

      Resolvable designs that block in two directions, by rows and by columns.

      row_column()
